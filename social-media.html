Social Media

Social media works best when content, paid distribution, audience learning, and conversion paths are connected. NewWavetrhy s.r.o. builds structured social systems for brands that want stronger visibility and more useful demand signals.

We treat social as a signal system, not a posting calendar. The work connects audience behavior, platform formats, paid distribution, retargeting logic, and message testing so the brand can understand what creates attention, trust, and movement toward conversion.

Service overview

This service organizes social activity around audience insight and business intent. It can cover paid social, organic direction, retargeting logic, creative testing, content rhythm, and reporting that explains what social is contributing.

What this service solves

  • Social channels that are active but commercially unclear
  • Paid social campaigns without consistent creative testing
  • Audience segments that are too broad or poorly matched to offers
  • Retargeting that repeats messages instead of moving users forward
  • Reports focused on surface metrics without useful decisions

What is included

  • Social channel and audience audit
  • Paid social campaign architecture
  • Audience testing and retargeting logic
  • Content rhythm and message direction
  • Creative testing recommendations for social formats
  • Reporting focused on engagement quality, conversion paths, and next actions

Expected business impact

  • Stronger paid social tests and retargeting sequences
  • Better use of audience signals for future campaigns
  • More consistent brand presence across formats
  • Social reporting that connects attention with business outcomes

FAQ

Do you manage organic social channels?

Yes, when it fits the scope. We can also provide strategy and systems for an internal team to execute.

Can this include Meta or LinkedIn advertising?

Yes. Paid social structure can be adapted to the platforms that match your audience and offer.

Do you focus on followers or conversions?

We prioritize business-relevant signals. Followers matter only when they support positioning, demand, or retention.

Can you improve an existing social setup?

Yes. We can audit current content, campaign structure, audience logic, and reporting before rebuilding the system.
