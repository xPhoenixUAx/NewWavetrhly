Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NewWavetrhy s.r.o. handles personal information collected through newwavetrhy.com and related business communications. Last updated: 3 May 2026.

1. Data controller

NewWavetrhy s.r.o. is responsible for personal data processed through this website and direct business inquiries. You may contact us at support@newwavetrhy.com for privacy-related questions.

2. Personal data we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when you contact us, including your name, email address, company name, service interest, budget range, and project details. We may also process basic technical data such as browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, and interaction data where analytics tools are used.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable under the GDPR, we process personal data on one or more of the following bases: your consent, performance of pre-contractual steps, legitimate interests in operating and improving our website and services, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and assess project fit.
  • To prepare proposals, scopes, and business communications.
  • To operate, secure, and improve newwavetrhy.com.
  • To understand website performance and content relevance.
  • To comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

5. Cookies and analytics

newwavetrhy.com may use essential cookies and privacy-conscious analytics or performance tools. Where required, non-essential cookies are used only with consent. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with trusted service providers such as hosting, email, analytics, CRM, or professional advisory providers when needed to operate the business. Such providers are expected to protect data appropriately and process it only for agreed purposes.

7. International transfers

If personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we aim to use appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or equivalent protections where required.

8. Retention

Inquiry and business communication data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including follow-up, project administration, legal compliance, and legitimate business records.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent, or request portability of your personal data. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal data. No website or electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact NewWavetrhy s.r.o. at support@newwavetrhy.com. Website: newwavetrhy.com.
