Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NewWavetrhy s.r.o. collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use newwavetrhy.com. Effective date: 1 January 2026. Last updated: 1 January 2026.

01. Who We Are

This Website is operated by NewWavetrhy s.r.o.

We provide digital marketing, advertising, and related business support services to companies across Europe and internationally.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), NewWavetrhy s.r.o. acts as the data controller for personal data collected through this Website.

We are committed to protecting the personal data of our website visitors, prospective clients, and business partners.

02. Data We Collect

We may collect personal data when you visit the Website, submit a contact form, communicate with us, or request services.

Types of data we may collect

  • Identity data: name, surname, company name, job title
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, business address
  • Financial data: billing details, invoicing information, VAT number where applicable
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, time zone, system data
  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, navigation paths, interactions
  • Marketing data: preferences for communications and engagement with campaigns
  • Business data: information related to services, such as goals, budgets, and project scope

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. If such data is provided, it will be handled appropriately and deleted where necessary.

03. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to:

  • respond to inquiries and contact requests
  • provide and improve our services
  • manage client relationships and communications
  • process invoices and business transactions
  • analyse website performance and user behaviour
  • support marketing and advertising activities
  • ensure website security and prevent misuse
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not use your data for unrelated purposes without informing you.

04. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Contract performance: when processing is necessary to provide requested services
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, improve, and secure our business and Website
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Consent: where required, such as marketing communications and cookies

You may withdraw your consent at any time.

05. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:

  • client and financial data: up to 7 years where legally required
  • inquiry data: up to 2 years
  • analytics data: up to 26 months
  • marketing data: until consent is withdrawn

We may anonymise data for analytical purposes.

06. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell personal data.

We may share data with service providers such as hosting, CRM systems, and email tools; advertising platforms such as Google and Meta; professional advisers such as legal and accounting providers; and authorities where required by law.

All third parties are required to process data securely and in compliance with applicable laws.

07. International Data Transfers

Some third-party providers, including Google and Meta, may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and approved data transfer mechanisms.

08. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, restrict processing, receive your data through data portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

09. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to ensure website functionality, analyse usage, and support marketing activities.

For full details, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or wish to exercise your rights, please contact:

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal changes, updates to our services, or changes in data processing practices.

Any updates will be published on this page.
