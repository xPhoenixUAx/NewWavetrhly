03/ Questions

Clear answers before the first conversation.

NewWavetrhy s.r.o. is built for focused collaboration. These are the questions that usually matter before deciding whether the fit is right.

What kind of companies does NewWavetrhy s.r.o. work with?

We work best with small and mid-sized companies, premium service businesses, founders, and growing brands that need a more structured approach to positioning, paid acquisition, landing pages, content, and reporting.

Do you only manage paid campaigns?

No. Paid campaigns are strongest when the strategy, creative, offer, landing page, and measurement system are connected. We can support the full growth system or improve one part when the surrounding structure is already strong.

Can you work with an existing internal team?

Yes. NewWavetrhy can operate alongside founders, marketers, designers, sales teams, or external specialists. The goal is to bring clearer direction, better execution rhythm, and reporting that helps the team make decisions.

What happens after the first inquiry?

We review the business context, current marketing activity, priorities, and constraints. If there is a strong fit, the next step is a focused scope: what needs to be built, improved, measured, and optimized first.
