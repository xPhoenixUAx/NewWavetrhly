01. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device, such as a computer, tablet, or mobile phone, when you visit a website. They are widely used to ensure websites function properly, improve user experience, and provide information to website operators.

Cookies can be session cookies, persistent cookies, first-party cookies, or third-party cookies. Session cookies are temporary and deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a defined period or until manually deleted. First-party cookies are set by our website, while third-party cookies are set by external services integrated into the website.

We may also use similar technologies such as web beacons, tracking pixels, local storage, and scripts. For the purposes of this policy, all such technologies are referred to as cookies.

This Cookie Policy forms part of our Privacy Policy. In accordance with the EU ePrivacy Directive and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

You can manage your preferences at any time via our cookie consent panel.

02. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to ensure the proper operation of the Website, including navigation, forms, and security; to remember your preferences and settings; to analyse how visitors use the Website and improve performance; to measure the effectiveness of marketing and advertising campaigns; to understand traffic sources and user behaviour; to support remarketing and retargeting campaigns; and to comply with advertising platform requirements such as conversion tracking.

03. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Website to function properly and cannot be disabled. They are typically set in response to actions such as setting privacy preferences, submitting forms, or navigating the website. Without these cookies, certain parts of the Website may not function correctly.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Website. They allow us to count visits, analyse user behaviour, and improve structure, content, and usability. The data collected is aggregated and does not directly identify individuals. You can opt out of analytics cookies at any time.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements, build audience profiles, and measure advertising performance. They are usually set by third-party advertising partners. These cookies track your browser across websites and may be used for personalised advertising. You can opt out of marketing cookies at any time.
