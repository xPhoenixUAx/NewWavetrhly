What this service solves

Many campaigns fail because they begin inside an ad account instead of inside a commercial strategy. NewWavetrhy s.r.o. builds paid acquisition around the offer, the audience, the creative argument, the landing experience, and the data needed to make confident decisions.

  • Paid media spend is active, but the brand does not know which message, audience, or offer is really working.
  • Campaigns generate traffic, but lead quality or conversion behavior is inconsistent.
  • Creative assets look acceptable, yet they do not express a strong reason to act.
  • Search and social activity exist in separate worlds with no shared funnel logic.
  • Reports show platform metrics but do not explain what should change next.

What is included

  • Acquisition audit covering funnel quality, offer clarity, tracking, platform structure, landing pages, and creative history.
  • Campaign architecture for paid social, search advertising, retargeting, and conversion paths.
  • Creative testing plan with message angles, format recommendations, hooks, proof points, and iteration cadence.
  • Landing page direction including wireframe notes, copy guidance, conversion sequence, and CTA strategy.
  • Analytics and reporting structure with campaign naming, event logic, dashboard priorities, and insight cadence.
  • Optimization cycles focused on audience response, cost quality, conversion movement, and commercial fit.

FAQ

No. It is useful before launch when the brand needs campaign architecture, tracking, and landing page logic before budget is committed.

No serious marketing partner should guarantee results without controlling the full commercial system. NewWavetrhy focuses on disciplined strategy, measurement, testing, and improvement.
